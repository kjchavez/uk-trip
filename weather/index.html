What to Pack

🧥 Layering Strategy

  • T-shirts for warm London days (75°+)
  • Long-sleeve + fleece for Cornwall coast (~58°)
  • Sweater + windbreaker for Edinburgh evenings
  • Pack one nicer layer for theatre night (May 27)

☂️ Rain Readiness

  • Packable waterproof jacket — non-negotiable
  • Compact umbrella for Edinburgh (Fri & Sun)
  • Waterproof bag cover for the camera
  • Quick-dry socks for Cornwall cliff paths

👟 Footwear

  • Walking shoes with grip — Cornwall cliffs & Edinburgh cobblestones
  • Avoid sandals: too cold for Scotland, too slippery on coastal paths
  • Comfortable everyday shoes for London (lots of walking)

🌬️ Wind Tips

  • Hat that won't blow off at Botallack Mine
  • Light scarf for Arthur's Seat & Calton Hill
  • Sunscreen — UV is high even on cloudy UK days
  • Sunglasses for the sunny first weekend
