## Discount & Deal Options

### 1. Friday Rush — £10 Tickets

- **Every Friday at 1:00 PM BST**, the NT releases £10 tickets for the following week
- First release: Friday 13 March at 1pm
- **For May 26-29 performances: try Friday 22 May at 1:00 PM BST**
- Released online at [tickets.nationaltheatre.org.uk](https://tickets.nationaltheatre.org.uk/)
- **Limit: Usually 2 tickets per transaction** — you'd need 2 people buying simultaneously for 3 tickets
- Extremely competitive — be logged in and ready at exactly 1:00 PM
- [Friday Rush info](https://www.nationaltheatre.org.uk/your-visit/ways-to-save/friday-rush/)

### 2. Entry Pass (ages 16-25)

- £10 tickets for young people
- [Entry Pass info](https://www.nationaltheatre.org.uk/your-visit/ways-to-save/entry-pass/)

### 3. Day Seats

- Small number of ~£20 seats released on the day of performance at the box office from 10:00 AM
- Risky for a group of 3

## Recommended Action

### Book NOW — this week if possible

**Why urgency matters:**
- Aidan Turner (Poldark star) + Lesley Manville + Marianne Elliott directing = high demand
- The show just opened (March 21) — as reviews come in, late-run dates sell out fast
- Booking online before April 1 avoids the new £4 transaction fee
- For 3 seats together in a good section, availability narrows quickly
- The NT does not charge booking fees for online purchases (before April)

**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to [nationaltheatre.org.uk/productions/les-liaisons-dangereuses](https://www.nationaltheatre.org.uk/productions/les-liaisons-dangereuses/)
2. Check performances for May 26-29 — target **Wed May 27 at 7:00 PM**
3. Book 3 tickets in mid-stalls or front circle (~£36-60/ticket = £108-180 total)
4. If May 27 is sold out, try Tue May 26 or Thu May 28 evening
5. **Backup plan:** Try Friday Rush on May 22 at 1:00 PM BST for £10 tickets
