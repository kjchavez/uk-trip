## Tips for Getting the Best Price

1. **Book 60-90 days ahead** - KAYAK data suggests booking ~63 days before departure is optimal for this route
2. **Be flexible on airports** - Also check Oakland (OAK) and San Jose (SJC) as departure airports, and London Gatwick (LGW) or Stansted (STN) as arrival
3. **Set price alerts** on [Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights) and [KAYAK](https://www.kayak.com)
4. **Compare nonstop vs. 1-stop** - A layover in Dublin or Reykjavik can save $100-200/person
5. **Check Tuesday/Wednesday departures** - Often cheaper than weekend flights
6. **Consider baggage fees** - Budget fares may not include checked bags; factor in ~$60-80 RT per bag
