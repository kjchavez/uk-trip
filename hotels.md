## Part 1: London (4 nights, ~May 23-27, 2026)

**Target area:** South Bank / Waterloo — walking distance to the National Theatre

### Top Picks

#### 1. Premier Inn London County Hall (Recommended)

- **Address:** Belvedere Road, London SE1 7PB
- **Distance to National Theatre:** ~0.3 miles (5-minute walk)
- **Room type:** Family room (double bed + sofa bed) — fits 3 adults
- **Price:** $97-$236/night; deals as low as $122 ([KAYAK](https://www.kayak.com/London-Hotels-Premier-Inn-London-County-Hall.327811.ksp))
- **3-night estimate:** $290-$710
- **Book:** [premierinn.com](https://www.premierinn.com/gb/en/hotels/england/greater-london/london/london-county-hall.html) | [Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/London-Hotels-Premier-Inn-London-County-Hall.h11785569.Hotel-Information) | [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g186338-d210361-Reviews-Premier_Inn_London_County_Hall_Hotel-London_England.html)
- **Pros:** Best location for the theatre, right on South Bank next to London Eye, reliable quality, breakfast available
- **Cons:** Family rooms book up fast; may need to call for 3-adult configuration

#### 2. Travelodge London Central Waterloo

- **Address:** 195-203 Waterloo Road, London SE1 8UX
- **Distance to National Theatre:** ~0.5 miles (8-minute walk)
- **Room type:** Family room (double + trundle). Note: officially 2 adults + 2 children — **may not suit 3 adults**
- **Price:** ~GBP 80-140/night
- **3-night estimate:** GBP 240-420 (~$300-530)
- **Book:** [travelodge.co.uk](https://www.travelodge.co.uk/hotels/207/London-Central-Waterloo-hotel)
- **Pros:** Budget-friendly, close to Waterloo station
- **Cons:** Trundle bed is child-sized; rooms are compact

#### 3. Travelodge London Central Southwark

- **Address:** 202-206 Union Street, London SE1 0LH
- **Distance to National Theatre:** ~0.6 miles (10-minute walk)
- **Price:** ~GBP 70-130/night (slightly cheaper than Waterloo)
- **3-night estimate:** GBP 210-390 (~$265-490)
- **Book:** [travelodge.co.uk](https://www.travelodge.co.uk/hotels/258/London-Central-Southwark-hotel)

#### 4. Park Plaza Westminster Bridge (Upgrade Option)

- **Address:** 200 Westminster Bridge Road, London SE1 7UT
- **Distance to National Theatre:** ~0.4 miles (6-minute walk)
- **Room type:** Studio rooms and suites with sofa beds can sleep 3
- **Price:** GBP 180-300+/night
- **Book:** [parkplaza.com](https://www.parkplaza.com/london-hotel-gb-se1-7ut/gbwestmi) | [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/gb/park-plaza-westminster-bridge.html)
- **Pros:** Modern, upscale, pool & gym, great location
- **Cons:** Significantly more expensive

**Note for 4 adults:** Most "family rooms" sleep 2 adults + 2 children. For 4 adults, consider: (1) calling the hotel to request a quad room or extra bed, (2) booking two interconnecting rooms, or (3) an Airbnb/serviced apartment near Waterloo with 2 bedrooms. An Airbnb may be the most comfortable and cost-effective option for 4 adults.

**Recommendation:** Premier Inn County Hall for best location, but call to confirm 4-adult capacity. Or search [Airbnb - Waterloo/South Bank, 4 guests](https://www.airbnb.co.uk/s/Waterloo--London/homes?adults=4) for a 2-bedroom flat.

## Part 2: Cornwall / Penzance (3 nights, ~May 29 - June 1, 2026)

**Target area:** Penzance and surroundings, near Poldark filming locations

### Top Picks

#### 1. YHA Penzance (Budget Pick)

- **Address:** Castle Horneck, Alverton, Penzance TR20 8TF
- **Room type:** Private family/triple room in a Georgian manor house
- **Price:** GBP 60-100/night (private room)
- **3-night estimate:** GBP 180-300 (~$225-375)
- **Book:** [yha.org.uk](https://www.yha.org.uk/hostel/yha-penzance)
- **Pros:** Beautiful historic building, gardens, kitchen access, very affordable
- **Cons:** 1 mile from town centre; some rooms share bathrooms

#### 2. The Queens Hotel Penzance

- **Address:** The Promenade, Penzance TR18 4HG
- **Room type:** Family/triple rooms available
- **Price:** GBP 90-150/night
- **3-night estimate:** GBP 270-450 (~$340-565)
- **Book:** [queenshotelpenzance.com](https://www.queenshotelpenzance.com/) | [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/gb/the-queens-penzance.html)
- **Pros:** Seafront location, sea views, bar & restaurant on-site
- **Cons:** Older building — quality varies by room

#### 3. Premier Inn Penzance (Long Rock)

- **Address:** Wharf Road, Long Rock, Penzance TR20 8AH
- **Room type:** Family room (same setup as London Premier Inns)
- **Price:** GBP 70-110/night
- **3-night estimate:** GBP 210-330 (~$265-415)
- **Book:** [premierinn.com](https://www.premierinn.com/gb/en/hotels/england/cornwall/penzance/penzance-a30-long-rock.html)
- **Pros:** Reliable, free parking, family room fits 3 adults
- **Cons:** 2 miles east of Penzance centre

#### 4. Airbnb / Holiday Cottage

- **Price:** GBP 80-150/night for a 1-2 bedroom property
- **Search:** [Airbnb - Penzance, 3 guests](https://www.airbnb.co.uk/s/Penzance--Cornwall/homes?adults=3)
- **Pros:** Kitchen (save on meals), more space, local feel
- **Cons:** Minimum stay requirements possible

Search [Airbnb - Penzance, 4 guests](https://www.airbnb.co.uk/s/Penzance--Cornwall/homes?adults=4). Alternatively, Premier Inn Penzance for easiest booking (call to confirm 4-adult capacity), or YHA for budget (book a 4-bed private room).

## Tips

- **Premier Inn** is the most 3-adult-friendly budget chain in the UK. Family rooms explicitly allow 3 adults.
- **Book on [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com) with "3 adults, 1 room"** filter to see only compatible rooms.
- **Book early** — late May is peak season start. Prices rise as availability drops.
- **Call the hotel** if the website doesn't show 3-adult options. Many can add a camp bed for GBP 15-30/night.
- **B&Bs in Cornwall often include breakfast** — better value than hotel + separate breakfast.
