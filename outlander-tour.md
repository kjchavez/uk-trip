## Option B: DIY Driving Route

### The Route (Clockwise Loop)

Total driving: ~3.5 hours. Total visit time: ~5-6 hours. Full day: ~9 hours.

```
Edinburgh → Midhope Castle (25 min)
    → Blackness Castle (10 min)
        → Culross (30 min)
            → Falkland (40 min)
                → Doune Castle (40 min)
                    → Edinburgh (1 hour)
```

### Car Rental
- Pick up from Edinburgh city center or airport the evening before or early morning.
- **Enterprise**, **Europcar**, **Arnold Clark** (Scottish company, good local options).
- ~£40-60/day. Book ahead.
- **Remember: drive on the left!** Roundabouts go clockwise.

## Hour-by-Hour Schedule (DIY)

---

### 8:00 AM — Depart Edinburgh

- Head west out of Edinburgh on the A90 toward the Forth Road Bridge.
- The route takes you across or alongside the Firth of Forth — look right for views of the iconic **Forth Rail Bridge** (a UNESCO World Heritage Site).
- **Before you leave:** Fill up with fuel, grab coffee and breakfast to go.

---

### 8:25 AM — Arrive at Midhope Castle (Lallybroch)

**Drive: Edinburgh → Midhope Castle** (25 min via A90 and Hopetoun Estate)

> **Important:** Midhope Castle is on the private **Hopetoun Estate**. You must enter via the Hopetoun House gates. Follow signs for "Midhope Castle" (not Hopetoun House itself). The estate typically charges ~£7.50pp for access.

### 8:30–9:30 AM — Midhope Castle

**Outlander: Lallybroch — Broch Tuarach, the Fraser family home**

This is **Jamie's ancestral home** — the tower where he grew up, where Claire and Jamie return after their wedding, and one of the most emotionally significant locations in the entire series.

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Entry** | ~£7.50pp (Hopetoun Estate access) |
| **Opening** | Check [hopetoun.co.uk](https://www.hopetoun.co.uk/) — seasonal hours, usually from 10 AM but sometimes earlier |
| **Access** | Exterior only (interior is derelict and unsafe) |
| **Parking** | Designated area near the castle |
| **Time needed** | 30-45 minutes |

**Scenes filmed here:**
- The gateway and front courtyard of Lallybroch (Season 1, Episode 12 onwards)
- Jamie and Claire's arrival at Lallybroch after their wedding
- The "Lady Broch Tuarach" scenes with Jenny and Ian
- Multiple return-to-Lallybroch scenes across seasons

**Photo tip:** Stand at the gate looking toward the tower — this is the classic "arriving at Lallybroch" shot from the show.

---

### 9:35 AM — Drive to Blackness Castle

**Drive: Midhope Castle → Blackness Castle** (~10 minutes)
- Head east back toward the coast. Blackness is signposted from the main road.

---

### 9:45–10:45 AM — Blackness Castle

**Outlander: Fort William — where Jamie was flogged by Black Jack Randall**

A dramatic 15th-century fortress jutting out into the Firth of Forth, shaped like a ship. One of the most atmospheric castles in Scotland.

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Entry** | ~£6-8.50pp (Historic Environment Scotland) |
| **Opening** | 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Apr-Sep). **Arrives at 9:45 — may need to wait for opening** |
| **Parking** | Small car park at the castle, free |
| **Link** | [historicenvironment.scot](https://www.historicenvironment.scot/visit-a-place/places/blackness-castle/) |
| **Time needed** | 45 min – 1 hour |

**Scenes filmed here:**
- Fort William — the British garrison where Jamie is imprisoned and flogged by Black Jack Randall (Season 1)
- The courtyard flogging scenes
- Jamie's escape from Fort William
- Various military garrison scenes

**What to see:**
- The main courtyard — stand where the flogging post would have been
- Walk the ramparts for views across the Firth of Forth
- The tower rooms used as prison cells in the show
- The atmospheric sea-facing walls

---

### 10:50 AM — Drive to Culross

**Drive: Blackness Castle → Culross** (~30 minutes)
- Head west along the coast, crossing the Kincardine Bridge. Culross (pronounced "COO-riss") is on the north shore of the Firth of Forth.

---

### 11:20 AM–12:30 PM — Culross

**Outlander: Cranesmuir — the village of Geillis Duncan**

A beautifully preserved 16th/17th-century village that looks almost exactly as it did 400 years ago. This is perhaps the most immersive Outlander location — you genuinely feel like you've stepped back in time.

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Village streets** | Free to explore |
| **Culross Palace & Garden** | ~£10pp (National Trust for Scotland) |
| **Opening** | Palace: 11:00 AM – 4:00 PM (check [nts.org.uk](https://www.nts.org.uk/visit/places/culross-palace)) |
| **Parking** | Small car park in the village, free |
| **Time needed** | 1 hour (village walk + palace garden) |

**Scenes filmed here:**
- **Culross Palace garden** = Claire's herb garden at Castle Leoch (where she makes medicines)
- **The Study** (the yellow building with the red pantile roof) = Geillis Duncan's house
- **The Mercat Cross and village streets** = Cranesmuir village scenes
- The witch trial scenes were filmed in the village square area

**What to see:**
- Walk the cobbled streets — they're the actual streets used in filming
- Find **The Study** (Geillis's house) — it's the most photogenic building in the village
- Visit the **Palace garden** — the herb garden where Claire tends her plants
- The **Town House** with its distinctive Dutch-gabled architecture

**Photo tip:** Stand at the Mercat Cross looking down the cobbled street toward the Firth of Forth — this is the classic Cranesmuir shot.

---

### 12:30–1:30 PM — Lunch

**Option 1: Lunch in Culross**
- **The Red Lion** — cozy pub right in the village. Traditional Scottish food.
- Limited options in tiny Culross — the pub is the main bet.

**Option 2: Lunch in Falkland** (eat at the next stop)
- More options in Falkland. Drive there first, then eat.

**Option 3: Picnic**
- Pick up sandwiches from a bakery before leaving Edinburgh and eat with views at any stop.

---

### 1:30 PM — Drive to Falkland

**Drive: Culross → Falkland** (~40 minutes)
- Head north across Fife. The drive passes through rolling Scottish countryside.

---

### 2:10–3:00 PM — Falkland

**Outlander: 1940s Inverness — where it all begins**

The charming village of Falkland doubles for **Inverness in the 1940s** — the setting for the very first scenes of the Outlander series, where Claire and Frank are on their second honeymoon after WWII.

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Village streets** | Free to explore |
| **Falkland Palace** | ~£18pp (NTS) — optional, not an Outlander location but a beautiful Renaissance palace |
| **Parking** | Car park in the village, free |
| **Time needed** | 45 min – 1 hour |

**Scenes filmed here:**
- **The Bruce Fountain** (in the village square) = where Frank sees Jamie's ghost staring up at Claire's window. *The* moment that sets the entire Outlander story in motion.
- **The Covenanter Hotel** = Mrs Baird's B&B where Claire and Frank stay
- **The village streets** = 1940s Inverness streets where Claire and Frank walk and shop
- The scene where Claire sees the Highlanders through the window

**What to see:**
- Stand at the **Bruce Fountain** — look up at the windows of the building across the square. This is Frank's perspective when he sees Jamie's ghost.
- Find the **Covenanter Hotel** — the exterior is Mrs Baird's B&B
- Walk the main street — it's remarkably unchanged from how it appears in the show
- **Falkland Palace** (optional) — a stunning Renaissance palace, former home of the Stuart monarchs. Beautiful gardens.

**Photo tip:** Recreate the "Frank sees the ghost" moment at the Bruce Fountain.

---

### 3:05 PM — Drive to Doune Castle

**Drive: Falkland → Doune Castle** (~40 minutes)
- Head west through Fife and into Stirlingshire. The castle is well-signposted from the A84.

---

### 3:45–5:15 PM — Doune Castle (The Grand Finale)

**Outlander: Castle Leoch — seat of Clan MacKenzie**

Save the best for last. Doune Castle is **Castle Leoch** — the castle where Claire is first taken after falling through the stones, where she meets Jamie, Colum, Dougal, and Mrs Fitz. This is the heart of Outlander's world.

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Entry** | ~£10pp (Historic Environment Scotland) |
| **Opening** | 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Apr-Sep, last entry 4:15 PM) |
| **Audio guide** | Included — **narrated by Sam Heughan** discussing the Outlander filming |
| **Parking** | Car park at the castle, free |
| **Link** | [historicenvironment.scot](https://www.historicenvironment.scot/visit-a-place/places/doune-castle/) |
| **Time needed** | 1–1.5 hours |

**Scenes filmed here:**
- **The Great Hall** = Castle Leoch's great hall where Claire meets Colum MacKenzie
- **The kitchen** = Mrs Fitz's kitchen where Claire is given food and clean clothes
- **The courtyard** = where Claire first arrives and meets the MacKenzies
- **The lord's chamber** = Colum's private rooms
- Various interior and exterior scenes throughout Season 1

**What to see:**
- Pick up the **Sam Heughan audio guide** at the entrance — he walks you through the castle discussing exactly what was filmed in each room, with behind-the-scenes stories
- Stand in the **Great Hall** — imagine it filled with MacKenzie clansmen
- Visit the **kitchen** — this is where Mrs Fitz first welcomes Claire
- Walk the **courtyard** — Claire's first view of Castle Leoch
- Climb to the **roof** for panoramic views of the surrounding countryside

**Bonus:** Doune Castle is also famous as the filming location for **Monty Python and the Holy Grail** — the audio guide has a separate Monty Python track if you want some laughs.

## Practical Notes

### Opening Hours Warning
- **Midhope Castle / Hopetoun Estate:** Seasonal hours. Check [hopetoun.co.uk](https://www.hopetoun.co.uk/) before your visit. Access is sometimes restricted.
- **Blackness Castle:** Opens at 10:00 AM. If you arrive before opening, adjust the schedule — visit Midhope first, then loop back.
- **Culross Palace:** Opens at 11:00 AM. The village streets are accessible anytime.
- **Doune Castle:** Last entry 4:15 PM. Don't arrive later than 3:45 PM.

### Booking Requirements
- **Guided tours:** Book 2-4 weeks ahead, especially in May.
- **Doune Castle:** No advance booking needed for individual visitors.
- **Culross Palace:** Check NTS website for any booking requirements.
- **Midhope Castle:** No booking, but check Hopetoun Estate is open.

### Driving Tips
- **Drive on the left.** This is the most important thing to remember.
- Roads between sites are good — mostly A-roads and motorways.
- The M9/M8 back to Edinburgh can have rush-hour traffic after 4:30 PM.
- Parking is free or cheap at all locations.
- A full tank of fuel will easily cover the ~120-mile loop.

### Mobile Signal & Navigation
- Signal is good on this route (closer to Edinburgh than rural Highlands).
- Download offline Google Maps for the area as backup.
- Sat nav / Google Maps will handle the route well.

### Weather
- Late May in central Scotland: expect 12-17°C (54-63°F), variable weather.
- Bring a waterproof jacket — castle courtyards and village streets are exposed.
- Blackness Castle is right on the water and can be windy.

### What to Bring
- Waterproof jacket
- Comfortable walking shoes (castle courtyards, cobbled village streets)
- Camera with charged battery
- Cash (some smaller sites are cash-only)
- This guide on your phone
- Snacks and water for the car
