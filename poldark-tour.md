---

### 8:45 AM — Depart Penzance for Botallack

**Drive: Penzance → Botallack** (~20 minutes)
- Head north on the A3071 toward St Just.
- At St Just, continue on the B3306 (the coast road) toward Pendeen.
- Turn left at the National Trust sign for **Botallack**.
- Park at the **National Trust car park** (£3-4, free for NT members).

> **Tip:** The B3306 coast road from St Just to Pendeen is one of the most dramatic roads in Cornwall. The landscape opens up to wild moorland and ocean — this is Poldark country.

---

### 9:00–10:30 AM — Botallack Mine & Crown Mines

**The iconic Poldark location.** The two clifftop engine houses perched above the crashing Atlantic are *the* image of Poldark — they doubled as **"Wheal Leisure"**, Ross's mine.

- **Walk from the car park** (~15-20 min downhill to the engine houses).
- The path is well-marked but uneven — sturdy shoes essential.
- Take in the Crown Mines engine houses from the clifftop viewpoint. This is where Poldark was filmed — no green screen, all real.
- Explore the ruins, read the interpretation boards about the real tin mining history.
- **Allow 1–1.5 hours** to walk down, explore, take photos, and return.

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Entry** | Free (open access) |
| **Parking** | National Trust car park, £3-4 |
| **Facilities** | None at the mine — use facilities at the car park |
| **NT link** | [nationaltrust.org.uk/botallack](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cornwall/botallack) |

**Poldark scenes filmed here:**
- Exterior shots of Wheal Leisure throughout the series
- Ross looking out over his mine
- Miners entering the mine workings
- Many of the dramatic clifftop scenes

### 10:30–10:45 AM — Drive to Levant Mine

**Drive: Botallack → Levant Mine** (~5 minutes)

---

### 10:45 AM–12:00 PM — Levant Mine & Beam Engine

A working Victorian beam engine — the oldest in Cornwall — that once pumped water from mine shafts running deep under the seabed.

- Tour the engine house and watch the beam engine in action (steaming days are typically Wednesdays and Fridays — **check ahead**, May 26 is a Tuesday so it may not be steaming).
- Even without steaming, the engine house and coastal setting are well worth a visit.
- **Walk the South West Coast Path** between Levant and Botallack (~1 mile along the cliffs). This is one of the finest stretches of the entire coast path — wild, windswept, Atlantic crashing below.

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Entry** | ~£10pp (free for NT members) |
| **Opening** | Usually 10:30 AM – 5:00 PM |
| **Parking** | National Trust car park on site |
| **NT link** | [nationaltrust.org.uk/levant-mine](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cornwall/levant-mine-and-beam-engine) |

> **Coast Path tip:** If you want to walk between Botallack and Levant on the coast path instead of driving, it's about 1 mile and takes 25-35 minutes. You can leave the car at Botallack, walk north to Levant, then walk back. Add ~1 hour for this option.

#### Option A: St Michael's Mount (Recommended if tide is right)

**Drive: St Just → Marazion** (~25 minutes via A30)

A fairy-tale tidal island with a medieval castle and subtropical gardens, visible across Mount's Bay from Penzance.

- **Critical: Check tide times!** You can walk across the causeway at low tide. At high tide, take the boat (£3pp return).
- Tide times for May 26, 2026: **Check [stmichaelsmount.co.uk](https://www.stmichaelsmount.co.uk/) closer to the date.**
- Allow 2-2.5 hours for the visit (walk across, climb to castle, explore gardens, return).

| Detail | Info |

| **Entry** | ~£18pp (castle + gardens) |
| **Opening** | Typically 10:30 AM – 5:00 PM (last entry 4:15 PM) |
| **Parking** | Marazion car park, ~£3-4 |
| **Book ahead** | Recommended in summer — [stmichaelsmount.co.uk](https://www.stmichaelsmount.co.uk/) |

**Back to Penzance:** Marazion is just 10 minutes from Penzance. Easy return by ~4:00 PM.

#### Option B: Holywell Bay (The Poldark opening credits beach)

**Drive: St Just → Holywell Bay** (~1 hour via A30 and A3075)

This is where **Ross gallops his horse** in the famous opening credits. A vast, dramatic beach backed by enormous sand dunes.

- Walk the beach and dunes. The scale is extraordinary.
- **Poldark connection:** The opening credits sequence, plus several beach scenes throughout the series.
- Allow 1-1.5 hours at the beach.
- Note: This is a longer drive, but worth it for devoted Poldark fans.

- Walk down to **Porthcurno Beach** — one of the most beautiful beaches in Cornwall with crystal-clear turquoise water.
- Visit the **Minack Theatre** — even without a show, the venue itself is extraordinary. The views from the seating are jaw-dropping.
- Check if there's a matinee or evening show — seeing any performance here is unforgettable.

## Practical Notes

### Driving in Cornwall
- **Drive on the left.** Roundabouts go clockwise.
- Cornwall's country roads are **narrow** — single-track with passing places in some areas. Pull into a passing place to let oncoming traffic through.
- The B3306 coast road has some tight sections but is well-maintained.
- Fuel up in Penzance before heading out — fewer stations in rural areas.

### National Trust Membership
- If you're visiting Botallack, Levant, and Holywell Bay, a National Trust membership (~£78/adult/year) could save money and includes free parking at NT car parks.
- You can join at any NT property.

### Mobile Signal
- **Signal is patchy on the Tin Coast.** Download offline Google Maps for the area before you leave Penzance.
- Botallack and Levant have limited or no mobile signal.

### What to Wear
- Waterproof jacket (essential)
- Sturdy walking shoes with grip (clifftop paths can be muddy)
- Layers — the coast is often windy even on warm days
- Sunscreen — the sea breeze disguises the sun

### Safety
- **Stay on marked paths** near cliff edges. The cliffs are eroded and the drops are sheer.
- Keep a safe distance from cliff edges, especially in wind.
- The coast path can be slippery when wet.
