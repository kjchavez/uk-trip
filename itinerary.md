## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1 -- Monday, May 25: Arrival in London

**Theme:** Settle in, explore the South Bank, get your bearings.

#### Morning / Early Afternoon
- **Arrive in London** (flights to Heathrow or Gatwick).
  - From **Heathrow**: Take the Piccadilly Line to Leicester Square, then Northern Line to Waterloo (~75 min, ~£5.50 on Oyster/contactless). Or Heathrow Express to Paddington (15 min, ~£25) then Bakerloo Line to Waterloo.
  - From **Gatwick**: Gatwick Express to Victoria (~30 min, ~£20) then Tube to Waterloo.
- **Check in** to hotel near Waterloo / South Bank.
- Freshen up and get oriented.

#### Afternoon
- **South Bank stroll**: Walk east along the Thames from your hotel.
  - Pass the **London Eye** (consider riding it for views, ~£30pp -- [book online](https://www.londoneye.com/) for discounts).
  - Walk past the **Royal Festival Hall** and **National Theatre** (scope out tomorrow's play venue).
  - Browse the **South Bank Book Market** under Waterloo Bridge -- outdoor stalls of second-hand books, open daily.
  - Continue to the **Tate Modern** (free entry) -- [tate.org.uk](https://www.tate.org.uk/visit/tate-modern). Even if art isn't everyone's thing, the Turbine Hall is spectacular and the views from the top floor cafe are excellent.

#### Evening
- **Dinner at Wahaca Southbank** -- Mexican street food, lively atmosphere, right on the river.
  [wahaca.co.uk](https://www.wahaca.co.uk/)
- Or **The Anchor & Hope** (gastropub, 36 The Cut, Waterloo) -- excellent British food, no reservations, arrive early. Great pies and roasts.
- Early night to recover from travel.

### Day 2 -- Tuesday, May 26: Classic London Sightseeing

**Theme:** Big London landmarks, with a Poldark/period drama thread for Mom.

#### Morning
- **Westminster walk**: Cross Westminster Bridge from South Bank.
  - See **Big Ben / Houses of Parliament** from outside.
  - Walk through **St James's Park** (beautiful in late May, flowers everywhere).
  - **Buckingham Palace** -- check if the Changing of the Guard is on (usually Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun in summer, but verify at [householddivision.org.uk](https://changing-guard.com/dates-buckingham-palace.html)). Tuesday may not have it, so don't count on it.

#### Late Morning
- **Churchill War Rooms** (optional, ~£28pp) -- [iwm.org.uk/visits/churchill-war-rooms](https://www.iwm.org.uk/visits/churchill-war-rooms). Fascinating underground WWII bunker. Book online.
- Or simply enjoy the parks and the walk.

#### Afternoon -- Period Drama London for Mom
- **Take the Tube to Greenwich** (Jubilee Line to North Greenwich, then bus, or the DLR to Cutty Sark -- more scenic).
  - **The Old Royal Naval College** -- used as a filming location in many period dramas and has Georgian/Baroque architecture that echoes the Poldark world. The Painted Hall (the "Sistine Chapel of the UK") is breathtaking and has appeared in countless films/TV shows.
    [ornc.org](https://www.ornc.org/) -- entry ~£15pp.
  - Walk through **Greenwich Park** for views of the Thames and Canary Wharf.
  - If time allows, pop into the **National Maritime Museum** (free) -- connects to Cornwall's maritime and mining heritage.

- **Alternative period drama option:** Visit the **V&A Museum** (free, South Kensington) -- the costume galleries have actual period garments that will thrill any Poldark fan. The 18th-century fashion rooms are exactly the era of Poldark.
  [vam.ac.uk](https://www.vam.ac.uk/)

#### Evening
- **Pre-theatre dinner near South Bank:**
  - **Skylon Restaurant** at the Royal Festival Hall -- modern British, river views. [skylon-restaurant.co.uk](https://www.skylon-restaurant.co.uk/)
  - Or **Tas Restaurant** (The Cut, Waterloo) -- affordable Turkish/Mediterranean, very popular pre-theatre spot. [tasrestaurants.co.uk](https://www.tasrestaurants.co.uk/)
- Evening walk along the Thames at dusk -- the South Bank lit up at night is magical.

### Day 3 -- Wednesday, May 27: The Big Day -- Les Liaisons Dangereuses

**Theme:** Relaxed morning, markets, then THE PLAY.

#### Morning
- **Borough Market** (open Wed-Sat) -- 10 min walk from hotel across the river.
  [boroughmarket.org.uk](https://boroughmarket.org.uk/)
  - Wander the stalls, sample British cheeses, pastries, and fresh food.
  - Great brunch spot: grab food from the stalls and eat by Southwark Cathedral.

#### Afternoon
- **Cross the Millennium Bridge** to **St Paul's Cathedral** (entry ~£23pp, or just admire from outside). [stpauls.co.uk](https://www.stpauls.co.uk/)
- Walk along the river back toward South Bank, or take the Tube back to Waterloo.
- **Rest and freshen up at the hotel** -- tonight is the main event!

- **For Mom especially:** Walk past the **Globe Theatre** (right by the Millennium Bridge). Even if you don't go in, the exterior is beautiful and it connects to the theatrical world she's about to experience. [shakespearesglobe.com](https://www.shakespearesglobe.com/)

#### Evening -- THE PLAY
- **5:30 PM:** Pre-theatre dinner.
  - **House Restaurant at the National Theatre** -- right in the building, convenient and good. [nationaltheatre.org.uk/your-visit/eat-and-drink](https://www.nationaltheatre.org.uk/your-visit/eat-and-drink)
  - Or grab something quick at one of the South Bank food stalls.

- **7:00 PM (or 7:30 PM):** **Les Liaisons Dangereuses** at the **National Theatre**
  - Starring **Aidan Turner** (Ross Poldark himself!) in a period drama on stage.
  - The play is set in 18th-century France -- aristocratic scheming, seduction, gorgeous costumes. Mom will be in heaven.
  - **Tip:** Arrive 30 min early to grab drinks at the NT bar and soak in the atmosphere. The building's brutalist terraces overlooking the Thames are iconic.
  - Check the [National Theatre website](https://www.nationaltheatre.org.uk/) for exact curtain time.

- **After the show:** Drinks on the South Bank. The NT has a bar that stays open after performances. Or walk along the river -- London at night from the South Bank is unforgettable.

### Day 4 -- Thursday, May 28: London to Cornwall by Train

**Theme:** Travel day -- but make it scenic.

#### Morning
- **Early checkout** from London hotel.
- Grab breakfast near the hotel or pick up pastries/coffee for the train.
- **Get to London Paddington station:**
  - From Waterloo: Bakerloo Line to Paddington (~15 min).
  - Aim to arrive 20-30 min before departure.

#### The Train Journey
- **GWR (Great Western Railway) London Paddington to Penzance**
  - Journey time: approximately **5 hours 15 minutes - 5 hours 30 minutes**
  - Recommended departure: **~9:00 AM or 10:00 AM** service (arrives Penzance ~2:15-3:30 PM)
  - The train passes through Reading, Exeter, Plymouth, and along the stunning Devon/Cornwall coast. **The stretch after Exeter is spectacular** -- the train runs right along the sea wall at Dawlish. Sit on the **left side** (facing forward) for sea views.
  - [Book advance tickets on GWR](https://www.gwr.com/) -- **Advance singles from ~£30-£60 per person** if booked well ahead. Standard walk-up fares can be £100+, so book early!
  - **Tip:** Book a table seat if available. Bring snacks, a book, and enjoy. The GWR buffet car sells drinks, sandwiches, and snacks.

#### Afternoon
- **Arrive in Penzance** around 2:30-3:30 PM.
- Penzance station is right in the town center.
- **Check in** to accommodation.
- **Explore Penzance on foot:**
  - Walk along the **Penzance Promenade** with views across Mount's Bay to St Michael's Mount.
  - Wander **Chapel Street** -- the old town's main street, full of character, antique shops, and pubs.
  - See the **Jubilee Pool** -- a stunning Art Deco seawater lido on the seafront. If it's warm enough, you can swim! [jubileepool.co.uk](https://www.jubileepool.co.uk/)

#### Evening
- **Dinner at The Shore** -- fresh seafood, harbor views, Penzance. [the-shore.co.uk](https://www.theshorepenzance.co.uk/)
- Or **The Turk's Head** -- Penzance's oldest pub (dates back to 1233!). Good pub food and local ales. Chapel Street.
- Or **Mackerel Sky Seafood Bar** -- tiny, brilliant, seasonal Cornish seafood. New Street, Penzance.

### Day 5 -- Friday, May 29: Poldark Day -- Botallack & The Tin Coast

**Theme:** The reason Mom is here. Windswept cliffs, dramatic mines, and Poldark magic.

#### Option A: Private Poldark Tour (~£425 for the group)
- A guide picks you up from Penzance and drives you to all the key filming locations.
- Book through [Poldark Tour Cornwall](https://www.poldarktour.com/) or similar operators.
- Typical tour covers: Botallack Mine, Levant Mine, the coastal paths, and other filming spots.
- Duration: typically a half-day or full-day experience.
- **This is the easiest option** -- no driving, expert narration, and they know all the exact camera angles.

#### Option B: DIY Poldark Locations (if not doing the guided tour)

**You will need a car for this option** -- rent one for the day from Penzance (Enterprise, Europcar, etc.) or use local buses (the #300 bus runs along the coast but is infrequent).

##### Morning -- Botallack Mine & The Tin Coast
- **Drive to Botallack** (~20 min from Penzance, heading north on the B3306).
- **Botallack Mine / Crown Mines** -- the iconic clifftop engine houses that served as "Wheal Leisure" and other mines in Poldark. This is THE image from the show.
  - Free to visit. Park at the National Trust Botallack car park (£3-4 parking).
  - Walk down the coastal path to see the engine houses perched above the Atlantic. About a 15-20 min walk from the car park.
  - [National Trust - Botallack](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cornwall/botallack)
  - Allow 1-1.5 hours to explore and take photos.

- **Levant Mine & Beam Engine** -- just up the coast from Botallack, a working Victorian beam engine.
  - [National Trust - Levant Mine](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cornwall/levant-mine-and-beam-engine)
  - Entry ~£10pp (free for NT members). The engine is steamed on certain days.
  - Allow 45 min - 1 hour.

- **Walk a section of the South West Coast Path** between Botallack and Levant. Wild, beautiful, and exactly what Mom pictures when she thinks of Poldark's Cornwall. About 1 mile between the two sites along the cliffs.

##### Afternoon -- More Poldark Coastline
- **Drive to St Agnes** (~40 min from Botallack) or stay on the Tin Coast.
  - **Wheal Coates** at Chapel Porth -- another dramatic clifftop mine ruin, also used in Poldark. Free to visit, National Trust car park.
  - [National Trust - Wheal Coates](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cornwall/wheal-coates)

- **Lunch near Botallack/St Just:**
  - **The Cook Book Cafe, St Just** -- lovely home-cooked food.
  - **Cape Cornwall Golf Club Cafe** -- simple but the views are extraordinary.
  - Or bring a picnic from a Penzance bakery and eat on the cliffs.

##### Late Afternoon
- Return to Penzance. If there's energy, visit **St Michael's Mount** (tidal island with a castle, accessed by walking across a causeway at low tide or by boat at high tide).
  - [stmichaelsmount.co.uk](https://www.stmichaelsmount.co.uk/)
  - Entry ~£18pp. Check tide times in advance!
  - The mount is at Marazion, 10 min drive or bus from Penzance.

#### Evening
- **Dinner at The Mexico Inn, Long Rock** -- good local pub between Penzance and Marazion. Hearty food.
- Or **Ben Tunnicliffe at the Sennen Cove** if you're still out west -- fresh seafood with sunset views.
- Or back in Penzance: **The Bakehouse** -- excellent restaurant on Chapel Street. [bakehouserestaurant.co.uk](https://www.bakehouserestaurant.co.uk/)

### Day 6 -- Saturday, May 30: Holywell Bay & North Coast

**Theme:** Beach, dunes, and the wilder side of Cornwall.

#### Morning
- **Drive to Holywell Bay** (~1 hour from Penzance, on the north coast near Newquay).
  - This is where **Ross gallops his horse along the beach** in the opening credits of Poldark.
  - A sweeping sandy beach backed by massive dunes. Stunning even on a grey day.
  - Walk along the beach, explore the sand dunes, and find **Holywell Cave** (accessible at low tide).
  - Free to visit. National Trust car park available (~£5-7).
  - [National Trust - Holywell Bay](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cornwall/holywell-bay)
  - Allow 1.5-2 hours for beach walking and exploring.

- **Tip for Mom:** Stand on the beach, look north along the sand, and imagine Ross Poldark on horseback. This is *the* spot.

#### Late Morning / Lunch
- **Drive to Padstow** (~30 min from Holywell Bay) for lunch.
  - **Rick Stein's Cafe** -- casual version of his famous restaurant, great fish. [rickstein.com](https://www.rickstein.com/restaurants/the-cafe/)
  - **Chough Bakery** -- award-winning Cornish pasties. The perfect quick lunch.
  - **Rojano's in the Square** -- Rick Stein's Italian place, good for a lighter meal. [rickstein.com/restaurants/rojanos-in-the-square](https://www.rickstein.com/restaurants/rojanos-in-the-square/)
  - Browse Padstow's harbor and shops.

#### Afternoon -- Choose Your Adventure
- **Option 1: Bodmin Moor & Lanhydrock House**
  - **Lanhydrock House** (National Trust) -- a grand Victorian country house with gorgeous gardens. Period drama heaven for Mom. ~45 min drive from Padstow.
  - [National Trust - Lanhydrock](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cornwall/lanhydrock)
  - Entry ~£18pp. Allow 2 hours.

- **Option 2: St Ives**
  - Drive to **St Ives** (~45 min from Padstow) -- beautiful harbor town, art galleries, narrow lanes.
  - **Tate St Ives** -- stunning gallery overlooking Porthmeor Beach. [tate.org.uk/visit/tate-st-ives](https://www.tate.org.uk/visit/tate-st-ives)
  - **Barbara Hepworth Museum & Sculpture Garden** -- magical. [tate.org.uk/visit/tate-st-ives/barbara-hepworth-museum](https://www.tate.org.uk/visit/tate-st-ives/barbara-hepworth-museum-and-sculpture-garden)
  - Get a cream tea (scone, clotted cream, jam) at one of the harbor cafes. **Cornwall rule: cream first, then jam** (not the Devon way!).

- **Option 3: Relaxed day**
  - Skip the long drive. Return to Penzance via the coast road and stop at **Porthcurno** -- a stunning turquoise cove. Visit the **Minack Theatre**, an open-air theatre carved into the cliffs above the sea. [minack.com](https://www.minack.com/) -- even if there's no show, the venue itself (£8pp) is jaw-dropping.

#### Evening
- **Last Cornish supper:**
  - **The Gurnard's Head** -- gastropub between St Ives and St Just. Excellent food, wild setting. [gurnardshead.co.uk](https://www.gurnardshead.co.uk/)
  - Or in Penzance: **Harris's Restaurant** -- long-established Penzance favorite, classic seafood. [harrissrestaurant.co.uk](https://www.harrissrestaurant.co.uk/)
  - Or a classic **fish and chips on the Penzance seafront** -- sometimes simple is perfect.

## Packing Tips for Cornwall in Late May

Cornwall in late May can be glorious -- but it is the Atlantic coast of England, so plan accordingly.

### Weather Expectations
- **Temperature:** 12-18C (54-65F) during the day. Can feel warmer in sun, cooler in wind.
- **Sunshine:** Late May is one of the best times -- long days (sunrise ~5:15 AM, sunset ~9:15 PM), often sunny, but rain is always possible.
- **Wind:** Coastal areas (especially Botallack, Holywell) can be very windy even on sunny days.

### Packing List
- **Waterproof jacket** -- not optional. A packable rain shell you can stuff in a daypack. Wind and rain can arrive fast on the coast.
- **Layers:** T-shirt + fleece/jumper + waterproof shell. The layering system is how locals survive.
- **Walking shoes or boots** -- coastal paths can be muddy, uneven, and steep. Proper grippy shoes are essential for Botallack and the cliff walks. Trainers/sneakers are not ideal.
- **Comfortable walking shoes for London** -- you will walk 8-12 miles a day.
- **Sunscreen and sunglasses** -- the Cornish sun can be surprisingly strong, and there's little shade on the coastal paths.
- **Small daypack/backpack** -- for water, snacks, camera, rain jacket on walking days.
- **Binoculars** (optional) -- seabirds, seals, and dramatic views. The coast is alive in late May.
- **Hat** -- sun hat for warm days, wool/fleece hat for windy clifftops. Seriously.
- **Smart-casual outfit** for the theatre night. The National Theatre doesn't enforce a dress code, but it's a special night -- dress up a little.
- **Swimsuit** -- for Jubilee Pool in Penzance or a brave dip at Holywell Bay (the water will be cold, ~12-14C, but it's exhilarating).
- **Reusable water bottle and tote bag** -- Cornwall is eco-conscious.

## Notes & Tips

1. **Book the theatre tickets ASAP.** Aidan Turner + National Theatre = high demand. Wednesday evening is a popular slot.
2. **Book train tickets 8-12 weeks ahead** for the best advance fares on GWR.
3. **Consider a National Trust membership** if you don't already have one (~£78/adult/year). It pays for itself in Cornwall -- Botallack, Levant, Wheal Coates, Lanhydrock, and many car parks are all NT.
4. **Tide times matter** for St Michael's Mount (causeway walking) and Holywell Bay caves. Check [tidetimes.org.uk](https://www.tidetimes.org.uk/) before those visits.
5. **Cornish cream teas:** The correct Cornwall method is jam first, then cream on top. Do not let anyone from Devon tell you otherwise.
6. **The trip tells a story:** London gives Mom the thrill of seeing Aidan Turner live on stage in a period drama. Cornwall gives her the wild, windswept world of Poldark made real. The train ride in between is the transition from one world to the other. It is going to be unforgettable.
