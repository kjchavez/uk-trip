Into Poldark Country

DIY Driving Tour of the Tin Coast

Tuesday, May 26
Base Penzance
Driving ~1.5 hrs
Walking 3–5 miles
Budget ~£30pp + car

"I am Ross Poldark, and this is my land."

8:45 AM — Depart Penzance

Drive to Botallack

Head north on the A3071 toward St Just, then continue on the B3306 coast road toward Pendeen. The landscape opens up to wild moorland and ocean — this is Poldark country. Turn left at the National Trust sign for Botallack.

Tip: The B3306 coast road from St Just to Pendeen is one of the most dramatic roads in Cornwall.
9:00 – 10:30 AM

Botallack Mine & Crown Mines

"Wheal Leisure" — Ross's mine

The iconic Poldark location. Two clifftop engine houses perched above the crashing Atlantic — the very image of the show. Walk from the car park (~15–20 min) down to the engine houses. No green screen, all real.

  • Exterior shots of Wheal Leisure throughout the series
  • Ross looking out over his mine
  • Miners entering the mine workings
  • Dramatic clifftop confrontation scenes
Entry Free
Parking £3–4 (NT)
Time 1–1.5 hours
Shoes Sturdy — uneven path
10:45 AM – 12:00 PM

Levant Mine & Beam Engine

Working Victorian beam engine

Just 5 minutes up the coast. Home to the oldest working beam engine in Cornwall, which once pumped water from mine shafts deep under the seabed. Walk the South West Coast Path between Botallack and Levant (~1 mile along the cliffs) for one of the finest stretches of the entire coast path.

Entry ~£10pp
Opens 10:30 AM
Time 45 min – 1 hr
Coast Path option: Walk between Botallack and Levant instead of driving — about 1 mile, 25–35 minutes. Wild, windswept, Atlantic crashing below.
12:00 – 1:00 PM

Lunch near St Just

Drive 10 minutes south to St Just for lunch. Try The Cook Book Cafe for home-cooked food, the King's Arms pub, or the Cape Cornwall Golf Club Cafe for extraordinary ocean views. Or pick up proper Cornish pasties from a bakery and eat on the cliffs.

1:15 PM onward

Afternoon: Choose Your Adventure

Pick one (or two if you're ambitious):

St Michael's Mount

Fairy-tale tidal island with medieval castle and subtropical gardens. Walk the causeway at low tide or take the boat.

25 min drive • ~£18pp • Check tide times!

Holywell Bay

Where Ross gallops his horse in the opening credits. A vast, dramatic beach backed by enormous sand dunes.

1 hr drive • Free • The Poldark fan choice

Porthcurno & Minack Theatre

Stunning turquoise cove and an open-air amphitheatre carved into the cliffs overlooking the sea.

20 min drive • ~£8pp • Jaw-dropping views

6:30 PM

Last Cornish Supper

The Bakehouse on Chapel Street for modern British cooking, The Shore for fresh seafood with harbor views, or classic fish & chips on the seafront — sometimes simple is perfect. Early night: you have a 7 AM train tomorrow!

Practical Notes

What to Bring

  • Waterproof jacket (essential)
  • Sturdy shoes with grip
  • Sunscreen & water
  • Offline Google Maps downloaded
  • Binoculars (coastal wildlife)

Driving

  • Drive on the left, roundabouts clockwise
  • Narrow Cornish lanes — use passing places
  • Fuel up in Penzance before heading out
  • Car rental ~£40–60/day from Penzance

Mobile Signal

Signal is patchy on the Tin Coast. Download offline maps before leaving Penzance. Botallack and Levant have limited or no signal.

Rain Backup

  • Eden Project — 50 min drive, ~£32pp
  • Tate St Ives — 20 min drive, ~£15pp
  • Minack Theatre — dramatic even in rain
  • Botallack is worth visiting in rain — very atmospheric

Into Outlander Country

Filming Locations Tour from Edinburgh

Friday, May 29
Base Edinburgh
Driving (DIY) ~3.5 hrs
Locations 5 key sites
Budget (DIY) ~£35–45pp

"You are my home now."

Option A: Guided Tour (Recommended)

No driving on the left, no navigation stress, and expert narration about filming and history. Small-group tours depart ~8:30–9:00 AM and return by ~5:30–6:00 PM.

Operator Price Group Size Notes
Rabbie's Tours ~£65–85pp Max 16 Award-winning. Departs St Andrew Square
Highland Explorer ~£79pp Small group Includes castle admissions
Viator options ~£50–95pp Varies Multiple operators, read reviews
Private tour ~£400–600 total Just you 4 Fully customizable — great for your group
Tip: A private tour at ~£100–150pp gives your group of 4 full control of the pace and is well worth considering. Book 2–4 weeks ahead.

Option B: DIY Driving Route

Clockwise loop from Edinburgh: ~3.5 hours driving + 5–6 hours visiting. Car rental ~£40–60/day.

8:00 AM — Depart Edinburgh

Drive to Midhope Castle

Head west on the A90 toward the Forth Road Bridge. Look right for views of the iconic Forth Rail Bridge (UNESCO World Heritage Site). Enter the Hopetoun Estate gates and follow signs for Midhope Castle.

8:30 – 9:30 AM

Midhope Castle

Lallybroch — Jamie's family home

Jamie's ancestral home — the tower where he grew up, where Claire and Jamie return after their wedding. One of the most emotionally significant locations in the series. Exterior access only (interior is derelict).

  • The gateway and courtyard of Lallybroch (Season 1, Episode 12 onwards)
  • Jamie and Claire's arrival after their wedding
  • The "Lady Broch Tuarach" scenes with Jenny and Ian
  • Multiple return-to-Lallybroch scenes across seasons
Entry ~£7.50pp
Access Exterior only
Time 30–45 min
Photo tip: Stand at the gate looking toward the tower — this is the classic "arriving at Lallybroch" shot from the show.
9:45 – 10:45 AM

Blackness Castle

Fort William — Black Jack Randall

A dramatic 15th-century fortress shaped like a ship, jutting into the Firth of Forth. This is where Jamie was imprisoned and flogged by Black Jack Randall.

  • Fort William garrison — Jamie's imprisonment and flogging
  • The courtyard flogging scenes (Season 1)
  • Jamie's escape from Fort William
  • Various military garrison scenes
Entry ~£6–8.50pp
Opens 10:00 AM
Time 45 min – 1 hr
11:20 AM – 12:30 PM

Culross

Cranesmuir — Geillis Duncan's village

A beautifully preserved 16th-century village that looks almost exactly as it did 400 years ago. Perhaps the most immersive Outlander location — you genuinely feel like you've stepped back in time. Pronounced "COO-riss."

  • Culross Palace garden = Claire's herb garden at Castle Leoch
  • The Study (yellow building with red roof) = Geillis Duncan's house
  • The Mercat Cross and village streets = Cranesmuir village
  • The witch trial scenes
Village Free to explore
Palace ~£10pp
Time ~1 hour
Photo tip: Stand at the Mercat Cross looking down the cobbled street toward the Firth of Forth — the classic Cranesmuir shot.
12:30 – 1:30 PM

Lunch

The Red Lion in Culross for traditional Scottish food, or carry on to Falkland for more options. Or pack sandwiches from Edinburgh and picnic with a view.

2:10 – 3:00 PM

Falkland

1940s Inverness — Where it all begins

The very first scenes of Outlander: Claire and Frank walking the streets of Inverness in 1945. This charming village doubles for the Highland capital and sets the entire story in motion.

  • The Bruce Fountain = where Frank sees Jamie's ghost staring up at Claire's window
  • The Covenanter Hotel = Mrs Baird's B&B
  • Village streets = 1940s Inverness
  • Claire sees the Highlanders through the window
Village Free to explore
Palace (optional) ~£18pp
Time 45 min – 1 hr
Photo tip: Recreate the "Frank sees the ghost" moment at the Bruce Fountain.
3:45 – 5:15 PM

Doune Castle — The Grand Finale

Castle Leoch — Clan MacKenzie

Save the best for last. This is Castle Leoch — where Claire is first taken after falling through the stones, where she meets Jamie, Colum, Dougal, and Mrs Fitz. The heart of Outlander's world. The audio guide is narrated by Sam Heughan himself.

  • The Great Hall = Castle Leoch's hall where Claire meets Colum MacKenzie
  • The kitchen = Mrs Fitz's kitchen
  • The courtyard = Claire's first arrival
  • Also famous for Monty Python and the Holy Grail!
Entry ~£10pp
Audio Guide By Sam Heughan
Last Entry 4:15 PM
Time 1–1.5 hours
5:15 PM

Drive Back to Edinburgh

~1 hour via M9 and M8. Arrive by ~6:15–6:30 PM with time to freshen up before a celebratory dinner.

7:00 PM

Celebratory Dinner

The Witchery by the Castle for dramatic Gothic fine dining, Whiski Bar for Scottish cuisine and 400+ whiskies on the Royal Mile, or a cozy Edinburgh pub — haggis, a dram of whisky, and stories from an unforgettable day.

Practical Notes

What to Bring

  • Waterproof jacket
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Camera with charged battery
  • Cash (some smaller sites are cash-only)
  • This guide on your phone

Driving

  • Drive on the left!
  • Good A-roads and motorways throughout
  • Full tank covers the ~120-mile loop
  • Rush-hour traffic on M9/M8 after 4:30 PM

Opening Hours

  • Midhope: check Hopetoun Estate seasonal hours
  • Blackness: opens 10:00 AM
  • Culross Palace: opens 11:00 AM
  • Doune: last entry 4:15 PM

Entry Fees Summary

~£35–45pp for all 5 key sites (excluding optional palaces at Falkland and Culross).

Explorer Pass from Historic Environment Scotland covers Blackness and Doune — worth it if visiting Edinburgh Castle too.
